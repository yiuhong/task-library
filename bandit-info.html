Reversal Learning

Two cards pay points at different rates. Pick the one that pays more often. During the main block the better card switches without a warning. Six practice trials come before the 48-trial main block.

What it shows

One option wins +10 points on about 80% of choices. The other wins +10 on about 20%. Every other outcome is +0. Practice does not reverse. The main block reverses the better option more than once. The results screen shows total points, how often the better option was chosen, how many reversals occurred, and a trial-by-trial strip of those choices.

Points are a teaching score. This is not a clinical or normed test.

Parameters

Practice
6 trials. The better side, or the better symbol, is chosen at random at the start. No reversal during practice. The practice screen shows the point total.
Main block
48 trials. Reversals are skipped on the first trial and when fewer than 4 trials remain.
Payoffs
High probability 0.80, low probability 0.20, win +10, otherwise +0. Feedback stays 750 ms, then a 280 ms blank.
Fixed interval
The default schedule. The next reversal is drawn from 15 through 18 trials after the last one, including both ends. The first gap is 16.
Performance criterion
Optional on the welcome screen. Reverses after at least 12 trials in the current phase when 8 of the last 10 choices were the better option, or when the phase reaches 18 trials.
Positions
Spatial deck is the default: the better option is a side, left or right. Randomized symbols put marks A and B on a new side each trial, and the better option is the symbol rather than the side.
Response time
Milliseconds from the moment both cards are ready until the choice.

Shells

The same reversal core runs in two shells. Probabilistic Reversal Learning is the gamified shell. Classical is the quiet lab shell. Classical uses the same 6 practice trials and the same main reversal block: the same probabilities, payoffs, reversal rules, and timings.

Each trial still shows +10 or +0, on practice and on the main block, in both shells. That payoff is the reward, so classical does not hide it and does not replace it with Correct or Incorrect. Classical does not show a live point total during trials, and it does not add an extra Correct or Incorrect about which option is better. The practice screen still shows the point total from those 6 trials. The end screen still reports total points, the better-option rate, and reversals. Those end metrics are the summary.

The saved payload and the CSV gain a mode field, gamified or classical, at the end. The localStorage key stays projectTrial_bandit_v1. A later finished run still replaces that payload.

How to play

  • Choose a card with the left or right pad, or with A or Left Arrow and L or Right Arrow.
  • A held key does not answer again. One choice is accepted while the cards are waiting.
  • Set the reversal schedule and the position mapping before Start Practice Block. New Session returns to that welcome screen.

Data

When the main block finishes, this browser stores one payload under the localStorage key projectTrial_bandit_v1. A later finished run replaces that payload. Nothing is uploaded. There is no Clear saved control.

Download CSV writes the current run, practice and main. Columns are trial, block, reversal_phase, high_prob_side, choice_side, chose_optimal, reward, rt_ms, timestamp, and mode. mode is gamified or classical and is the last column. The file name is bandit_reversal_ plus a timestamp. The page does not log orientation turns.

Digital Corsi Block-Tapping Task

You will see 9 blocks arranged on the board. On each trial, a sequence of blocks will flash in neon violet one at a time.

Nine identical blocks light one at a time. When the sequence ends, tap the blocks back in the same order.

01. Watch carefully: Wait until the entire flashing sequence finishes.
02. Wait for the "GO" cue: An audio-visual signal indicates when recall begins.
03. Reproduce the order: Tap the blocks in the exact same order. The trial auto-submits once you reach the sequence length, or you can press Clear to reset your current input before finishing.
04. Progression: Begins with a 2-trial Warmup (Span 3), followed by the Main Assessment starting at Span 3 and increasing up to Span 9.

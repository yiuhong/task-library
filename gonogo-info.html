Go / No-Go

A green arrow is a go. Tap the matching side before the deadline. A red X is a no-go. Do not tap. Eight practice trials come before the 40-trial main block.

What it shows

Most trials are go trials. A smaller share are no-go trials. The results screen reports accuracy, mean go response time, commission rate, and omission rate. A commission is a tap on a no-go trial. An omission is a go trial that reaches the deadline with no tap.

A correct go shows HIT. The wrong side shows WRONG SIDE. A tap on a no-go shows FALSE ALARM. A missed go shows TOO SLOW. A withheld no-go shows WITHHELD. This is a teaching demo, not a clinical or normed test.

Parameters

Practice
8 trials. round(8 × 0.75) = 6 go and 2 no-go. Go sides alternate left and right before the shuffle, so 3 left and 3 right. No-go is 1 left and 1 right in the stored side field. The picture on a no-go trial is the red X, not an arrow.
Main block
40 trials. 30 go and 10 no-go. Go is 15 left and 15 right. No-go stores 5 left and 5 right. Order is a Fisher–Yates shuffle.
Fixation
400 ms. A plus sign.
Go window
700 ms. A lime arrow and a shrinking bar. The correct pad matches the arrow. A miss is an omission.
No-go window
800 ms. A coral X. The correct action is to do nothing until the window ends. A tap is a commission.
Feedback
400 ms, then a blank from 250 ms through 450 ms.
Response time
Milliseconds from stimulus onset on a tap. A withhold stores an empty time. Go mean response time uses correct go trials only.

Classical presentation

The welcome and results screens have a Gamified | Classical control. The choice is stored in this browser as project-trial:mode. ?mode=classical still opens the lab shell.

No additional go / no-go citation was verified for this shell. The go stimulus stays a green arrow and the no-go stimulus stays a red X. The glowing frame and halo around the X are removed. Trial counts, the 700 ms go window, and the left/right mapping are unchanged.

How to play

  • On a green arrow, tap the pad on that side, or press A or Left Arrow for left and L or Right Arrow for right.
  • On a red X, keep still until the trial ends.
  • A held key does not answer again. One tap is accepted while the stimulus is waiting.
  • Run New Session starts practice over.

Data

When the main block finishes, this browser stores one payload under the localStorage key projectTrial_gonogo_v1. A later finished run replaces that payload. Nothing is uploaded. There is no Clear saved control.

Download CSV Log writes the current run, practice and main. Columns are trial, block, trial_type (GO or NOGO), target_side, response, correct, rt_ms, and timestamp. The file name is projectTrial_gonogo_ plus a timestamp. The page does not log orientation turns.
